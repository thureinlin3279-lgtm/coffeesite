Featured Coffee

Single Espresso

Tasting Notes: Pure coffee essence with bright acidity, rich crema, and immediate flavor impact delivering an intense coffee experience
How to Make: Use 7-9g of finely ground coffee with 25-30ml of water heated to 90-96°C. Extract for 25-30 seconds under proper pressure
$4.50

Classic Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Perfect harmony of bold espresso, creamy steamed milk, and luxurious microfoam creating a balanced, velvety experience
How to Make: Start with a single espresso shot, add equal parts steamed milk and microfoam. Steam milk to 65°C for silky texture
$5.25

Hot Americano

Tasting Notes: Clean and smooth with bright acidity, warm and approachable with subtle coffee complexity that's easy to enjoy
How to Make: Pour one shot of fresh espresso into a cup, then add 120-150ml of hot water (85-90°C). Serve immediately while hot
$3.75

Lungo

Tasting Notes: Milder intensity with extended extraction, slightly more bitter with complex flavor development and aromatic depth
How to Make: Use double the amount of water compared to normal espresso.Extract for 45-60 seconds to achieve the perfect lungo balance
$4.25

Mocha Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Decadent blend of rich chocolate and coffee with sweet cocoa undertones balanced by subtle coffee bitterness
How to Make: Add 1-2 tablespoons of chocolate syrup to espresso, then add steamed milk and foam. Dust with cocoa powder for finishing touch
$6.00

Iced Americano

Tasting Notes: Refreshingly crisp with clean coffee flavor, subtle bitterness,and invigorating coolness perfect for any time of day
How to Make: Fill glass with ice, add one shot of espresso, then pour cold water to fill. Stir gently and serve with a straw
$4.00
