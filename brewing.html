Brewing Equipment

Drip coffee maker Machine

An automatic machine that pours hot water over ground coffee using a filter. Common in homes and offices. Easy and convenient for multiple cups.

French Press

A manual brewer where coarse coffee grounds steep in hot water for several minutes before pressing down a metal plunger to separate the grounds. Rich and full-bodied coffee.

Espresso Maker Machine

Forces hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure to create a small, strong shot of espresso. Comes in manual, semi-automatic, and fully automatic versions.

Pour Over

A cone-shaped device where hot water is poured manually over grounds in a circular motion. It drips through a paper filter into a carafe or mug. Offers clean, aromatic brews.

Aero Press

A compact, travel-friendly device that uses air pressure to force coffee through a filter. Brews rich, smooth coffee with low acidity in about 1-2 minutes.

Siphon Pot

Uses heat and vacuum pressure to brew coffee in a visually dramatic way. Produces a clean and vibrant flavor profile. Popular with enthusiasts.

Percolator

A classic stovetop or electric brewer that continuously cycles boiling water through coffee grounds. Makes strong, often bitter coffee.

MokaPot

The Moka Pot brews strong, espresso-like coffee by forcing boiling water through finely ground coffee under pressure. It’s typically used on a stovetop and delivers a rich, concentrated shot of coffee.

Turkish coffee Pot

A small metal pot used to brew extra-fine coffee grounds with sugar over low heat. Coffee is unfiltered and strong with a thick foam. Traditional in Middle Eastern cultures.
