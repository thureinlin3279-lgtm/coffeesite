Tasting Notes: Hot/cold contrast, creamy & sweet with rich vanilla undertones
How to Make: Pour a fresh shot of espresso over a scoop of premium vanilla ice cream. Serve immediately for the perfect temperature contrast.

lungo

Tasting Notes: Milder intensity with extended extraction, slightly more bitter with complex flavor development
How to Make: Use double the amount of water compared to normal espresso. Extract for 45-60 seconds to achieve the perfect lungo balance.

Double Espresso

Tasting Notes: Intensely robust with full body, rich crema, and deep aromatic complexity
How to Make: Use approximately 18g of finely ground coffee with 50-60ml of water. Extract under 9 bars of pressure for 25-30 seconds.

$3.20

Espresso Macchiato

Tasting Notes: Bold espresso intensity softened by a delicate cloud of velvety milk foam
How to Make: Prepare a single espresso shot and top with exactly one teaspoon of perfectly steamed milk foam. The foam should sit gently on top.

$2.80

Ristretto

Tasting Notes: Highly concentrated with natural sweetness, minimal bitterness, and intense aromatic profile
How to Make: Use the same amount of coffee as espresso but extract with only half the water (15ml). Stop extraction at 15-20 seconds for optimal concentration.

$2.70

Single Espresso

Tasting Notes: Pure coffee essence with bright acidity, rich crema, and immediate flavor impact
How to Make: Use 7-9g of finely ground coffee with 25-30ml of water heated to 90-96°C. Extract for 25-30 seconds under proper pressure.

$2.50
