Our Coffee Selection

Classic Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Perfect harmony of bold espresso, creamy steamed milk, and luxurious microfoam creating a balanced, velvety experience
How to Make: Start with a single espresso shot, add equal parts steamed milk and microfoam. Steam milk to 65°C and create silky microfoam texture.

$4.50

Dry Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Pronounced espresso character with airy foam texture, less creamy but more coffee-forward intensity
How to Make: Prepare espresso and top with abundant microfoam while minimizing steamed milk. Ratio should be 1:1:3 (espresso:milk:foam).

$3.50

Wet Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Silky smooth texture with enhanced creaminess, mellow coffee flavor balanced by rich steamed milk
How to Make: Use more steamed milk and less foam than traditional cappuccino. Ratio should be 1:3:1 (espresso:milk:foam) for maximum creaminess.

$4.0

Iced Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Refreshingly cool with smooth coffee flavor, creamy texture, and invigorating chill perfect for warm weather
How to Make: Pour fresh espresso over ice, add cold frothed milk, and top with chilled foam. Serve immediately in a chilled glass.

$4.80

Mocha Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Decadent blend of rich chocolate and coffee with sweet cocoa undertones balanced by subtle coffee bitterness
How to Make: Add 1-2 tablespoons of chocolate syrup to espresso, then add steamed milk and foam. Dust with cocoa powder for finishing touch.

$6.50

Cinnamon Cappuccino

Tasting Notes: Warming spice blend with aromatic cinnamon notes complementing rich coffee, creating a cozy and comforting experience
How to Make: Prepare classic cappuccino and generously dust the foam with ground cinnamon. Add a cinnamon stick for stirring and extra aroma.

$5.50
