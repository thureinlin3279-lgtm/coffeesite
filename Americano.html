Our Coffee Selection

Hot Americano

Tasting Notes: Clean and smooth with bright acidity, warm and approachable with subtle coffee complexity that's easy to enjoy
How to Make: Pour one shot of fresh espresso into a cup, then add 120-150ml of hot water (85-90°C). Serve immediately while hot.

$4.50

Iced Americano

Tasting Notes: Refreshingly crisp with clean coffee flavor, subtle bitterness, and invigorating coolness perfect for any time of day
How to Make: Fill glass with ice, add one shot of espresso, then pour cold water to fill. Stir gently and serve with a straw.

$4.0

Long Black

Tasting Notes: Rich crema preservation with stronger coffee flavor, more aromatic intensity than traditional Americano
How to Make: Pour hot water into cup first, then carefully pour espresso on top to preserve the crema layer. This maintains the coffee's aromatic oils.

$5.50

White Americano

Tasting Notes: Smooth and mellow with creamy texture, gentle coffee flavor softened by milk's natural sweetness
How to Make: Prepare a standard Americano and add a splash (15-30ml) of steamed or cold milk according to preference. Stir gently to combine.

$5.0

Iced Honey Americano

Tasting Notes: Natural sweetness with floral honey notes, smooth finish that complements coffee's natural flavors without overpowering
How to Make: Dissolve 1 teaspoon of quality honey in hot espresso first, then add ice and cold water. Stir well to ensure honey is fully integrated.

$4.50

Sparkling Americano

Tasting Notes: Effervescent and zesty with bright coffee flavors enhanced by carbonation, creating a unique bubbly coffee experience
How to Make: Pour espresso over ice, then slowly add chilled sparkling water to maintain carbonation. Garnish with lemon twist for extra zest.

$4.0
